🧩 Problem Statement
Goal:
To segment customers into distinct groups based on their transaction behavior and demographic/card data, using unsupervised learning techniques. The aim is to discover hidden patterns in customer behavior to help the business tailor marketing, detect anomalies, or improve product offerings.

🔍 Why? (Business Context)
Financial institutions and businesses often have thousands of customers, each with different behaviors. But without labels like "loyal", "high risk", or "deal seeker", it becomes hard to:
Know which customers to reward or retain
Spot potentially risky or dormant customers
Create targeted offers for different groups
So we’ll use clustering to automatically identify segments based on actual customer behavior.

💡 What Will We Do?
We will:

Preprocess raw data from transactions, customer profiles, and card usage
Engineer behavioral features like:
Transaction frequency
Average spend
Recency of last transaction
Diversity in card usage
Demographics (age group, region, etc.)
Apply clustering (e.g., KMeans or DBSCAN) to group customers


Analyze each cluster to give them a business-friendly label like:
“Frequent Spenders”
“Dormant Users”
“Low Volume, High Value”
“Infrequent but Loyal”

Visualize the clusters using 2D projection (PCA or t-SNE)
(Optional) Recommend actions for each group

🎯 Expected Outcome
3–6 meaningful customer segments
Visuals showing how customers group together
A profile table summarizing each segment’s characteristics
Business insights that could influence marketing or product decisions
